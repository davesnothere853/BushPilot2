  (b) Our own marching-cubes layer just for underground pockets (mine

  (c) Hybrid: keep the heightfield surface, add voxel "cave volumes" you
      break into from a pit wall.
- Also opens: hard-rock mining tunnels (adits), old 1890s workings to
  find, flooded caves (ties to groundwater idea #62).
- NOT building yet - needs Dave's pick after the small-map port works.
- Dave add-on: "i want to dig very deep". Today bedrock (2.5-4 m down)
  stops every dig. Deep digging needs: bedrock that CAN be broken (slow,

  walls that need benching to stay up. A heightfield can do very deep OPEN
  PITS; tunnels/shafts/caves need voxels (see above) - deep digging +
  caves together are a strong case for option (a) voxel terrain.
- Dave add-on: "eventually put supports up under ground" - timber mine

=== 70. Realistic suspension + driving physics (2026-10-10) ===
Dave: "i want realistic suspension and driving physics if possible"
- Today the dozer/excavator drive KINEMATICALLY: they slide along and snap
  to the ground height (no mass, no traction, no tipping). The plane is
  the only thing with real physics (custom rigid body, sprung gear).
- Plan: give ground machines the same treatment as the plane - a custom
  rigid body (mass, inertia) with spring/damper contact points:
  * tracked (dozer, excavator): 6-8 road-wheel contacts per track, each a
    raycast spring+damper on the ground; per-track drive force with
    traction from the surface (gravel/grass/mud/bog), slip and spin-out,
    pivot turns by braking one track; climbs limited by real grip and
    weight transfer; can tip over on a side-slope; blade load adds drag.
  * wheeled (trucks, loaders, the fuel bowser later): raycast wheels with
    spring/damper/anti-roll, Pacejka-lite tyre friction, differential.
  * muskeg: soft ground sinks contacts and saps traction (storyboard).
- Built in our own GDScript physics (like plane.gd) rather than Godot's
  VehicleBody3D: we control every number and it reads the deformable
  ground directly (and the voxel ground later via raycasts).
- Ties into BeamNG-ish damage later (bent tracks, broken springs).
- Dave: voxel ground must NOT be blocky - smooth, like other voxel games /
  Farming Simulator / Out of Ore. => Voxel Tools SDF channel meshed with
  VoxelMesherTransvoxel (smooth marching-cubes style), never the blocky
  mesher. Surface stays smooth hills; digs leave smooth pits and tunnels.

237. THE STORY: HOW IT STARTS (Dave, 14 Oct). The opening campaign:
  1. You step out of the cabin door after spawning in. Lightning strikes a
     tree across the runway from you: you stumble, throw a hand up against
     the flash. The wind picks up; the fire spreads, burning everything in
     its path toward the airport.
  2. Your only way out: the plane in the hangar, in the fire's path. Get to
     it, get it out, take off through the smoke and gusts - out just in time.
  3. Land at another airport, charred but safe.
  4. Later you drive back through the burnt forest after the fire to clean
     up: most of it's gone - your house and everything in it. Except an old
     shed with a beaten-up retired bush plane you've been trying to restore.
  5. You start over in the shed: a cot, a stove and the wreck. Delivery
     missions in your truck to save enough to fix the plane.
  6. The plane fixed, the maiden voyage: off the cliff strip (Eagle Rock),
     you barely make it off the ground, drop off the edge - then pull out of
     it and come into view rising through the sky, into the sunset.

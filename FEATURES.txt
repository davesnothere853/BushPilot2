BUSH PILOT 2 - WHAT'S IN THE GAME AND HOW TO USE IT
(test bed; updated 2026-10-11. Keys are the defaults - Menu > Controls lets
you rebind any of them. "Pad" = Xbox-style controller.)

=============================================================================
 CHANGES IN THIS UPDATE
=============================================================================
 - Machines no longer fall through the map when you dig deep and drive into
   the hole: the whole body of every machine/truck now collides with the dirt
   (before only the wheels/tracks did). Very deep narrow shafts: the machine
   nose-dives in but stays out of the walls.
 - Parked / for-sale planes sit on their wheels instead of floating, and are
   real planes (physics running, can be bought and flown).
 - Plane door: opening it shows the real cabin (seats, panel, yokes) instead
   of a black box. Every plane has its cabin built, not just yours.
 - The boxy shape sticking out of the roof behind the wing is gone (the cabin
   inside was poking through the tapering tail).
 - Gauges easier to see (plane and both trucks): faces glow a little, needles
   always bright, glass no longer a mirror. Water truck dash rebuilt so every
   gauge is in view and nothing blocks it (3-spoke wheel, lower gear knob,
   dash below the hood line). Pickup: the cab was a solid block with a lid
   over the dash - now hollow; hood lowered to the real truck's height so you can
   see over the nose. Plane: resting view looks a little lower so the top of
   the panel shows.
 - Water truck: diesel tank now lies along the frame under the driver's door
   with the steps in front (was a drum sticking out sideways); air tanks along
   the rails (were through the drive shaft); air-intake pipe connected.
 - Cockpit camera: head locked at the pilot's eye, mouse = free look, the view
   stays where you leave it. A dot in the middle works switches (look at it,
   click). Hold Alt for a normal mouse pointer. Hold right mouse to zoom in on
   a gauge. Mouse wheel no longer zooms the view out (that's what made it feel
   like you slid back in the seat). Seat-back (End) limited to 6 cm.
 - First person on foot: your own body no longer shows/clips into view (it
   still casts its shadow).
 - Camera shake: engine buzz, rumble on the ground (rougher off the strip),
   a bump on touchdown, stall buffet, explosions. Menu > Options > Camera
   shake (0% turns it off, up to 200%).
 - F10 also opens the menu (Escape can be grabbed by other apps).
 - NEW PICKUP: built from scratch in Blender over scale drawings - frame,
   V8, transmission, transfer case, driveshafts that spin, front I-beams and
   coils, rear axle on leaf springs, exhaust, tank, spare. Clear glass, see-
   through lamp lenses with working bulbs (head, park, tail, brake, reverse,
   blinkers, markers, plate), bench seat with a Mexican blanket, steering
   wheel that turns, opening hood and tailgate, tyre choices.
 - Everything checked to scale against the pilot (1.76 m): pickup 6.0 m long
   1.92 m tall, water truck, dozer (cab raised so you fit), loader,
   excavator, forklift and plane all in proportion.
 - Sound: pickup sounds like an old cammed 350, water truck a diesel that
   revs up when the PTO pump goes on, machines with hydraulic whine; wind,
   birds, creek, rain, crickets, muffled inside cabs.

=============================================================================
 GOLD, DIGGING AND TUNNELS (inside the test square)
=============================================================================
 - Shovel and pick are real tools (hotbar). Dig with E where you look; hard
   or frozen ground needs the pick. Pan at water: carry dirt, E at the water.
 - Ground is generated from the world seed: rolling ground, rock knoll, bog,
   pond, creek, buried pay channels (richest on the inside of bends), a
   bench terrace, the mining pad. Console: newworld / seed.
 - Finds near the surface: bottles, pans, 1898 coins - sell them with gold.
 - Tunnels: dig your own; timber sets hold the roof; ladders to climb out.
 - Generator (fill with the gas can, E start/stop, Y pick up) powers rope
   lights strung along drifts.
 - Water pump: set it at the water's edge (E), runs to the lowest ground.
 - Sluice box at the creek; wash plant / trommel on the pad (runs on gas:
   pour a gas can into its drum, E start/stop); dump dirt into the hopper with
   the excavator or loader. Gold builds up; sell at the menu.
 - Gas can: fill at the fuel pumps at town strips (E), pour into machines.
 - Workbench at the tool yard: Y picks the recipe, E makes it (timber set,
   ladder, sluice and more) from logs you cut.
 - Chainsaw: fell trees (E at a tree) for logs.
 - Fire: campfires, and wildfire that spreads through what grows there.

=============================================================================
 MACHINES (walk to the cab door, O to get in / out)
=============================================================================
 All drive with W A S D (S brakes, then reverses), Space handbrake.
 Dozer:      E blade down/up, Shift/Ctrl raise/lower by hand. Push with the
             blade down to cut and fill: roads, pads, ramps.
 Excavator:  look where to dig (the arm follows), E dig / dump. Y switches to
             levers: boom, stick, bucket by hand. Dump into the hopper.
 Loader:     Shift/Ctrl arms up/down, R/F curl/dump bucket, E swap bucket and
             forks. Drive into dirt with the bucket flat to fill it.
 Forklift:   Shift/Ctrl forks up/down, E pick up / set down (pallets, DC-3).
 Pickup:     Generic late-80s 3/4-ton SuperCab long bed (modelled in
             Blender off scale drawings). E hitch / unhitch (back the ball
             under a tongue). Load cargo into the bed on foot at the tailgate.
             I engine (cammed V8 sound). V headlights, Z / X left / right
             blinker, brake and reverse lights work. On foot: Y at the nose
             opens/closes the hood, Y at the back the tailgate.
             Working gauges (MPH, RPM, fuel, temp, oil, amps), 4-speed.
             Parts: everything from the engine to the lockers, lift and
             tyres (235 highway, 31 A/T, 33 mud, 35 mud) is a real part -
             Esc -> Workshop on the apron, or by hand under the hood.
 Water truck: late-80s heavy truck with a 4,000 gal tank. I engine on/off (needs air
             to build before it drives). E fills at water (park the suction
             hose at the back-left by the pond, creek or a flooded hole) /
             switches the sprays. 1-5 open/close the spray valves (FL FR MID
             RL RR). On foot at the back: E takes the hose off the reel, open
             the HOSE valve on the manifold (E at it), E opens the nozzle; Y
             reels it back in. 50 ft hose.
 Fuel truck: the same truck with a diesel/avgas tank. E at a fuel pump
             loads it; anywhere else E fills whatever's parked close by
             (plane and pickup avgas, water truck diesel).
 Mud:        open diffs (a spinning wheel takes the power), 4WD and lockers
             to get through; a mud bog near the strip to play in.
 Gauges in all the trucks work like the plane's. The pickup has a
             three-gauge pod under the dash and the plane a two-gauge pod
             under the panel: wire each gauge to a reading (boost, fuel
             pressure, air/fuel, tach, temps...) in Esc -> Workshop.

=============================================================================
 BUILDING, JOBS, WORLD
=============================================================================
 - B: lay out your own strip, put up a log cabin, steel pole barn / hangar,
   or a camp. Hangar doors: E at the door.
 - J: job board - passengers, mail, groceries, fuel drums, pallets; cargo is
   real objects you load. Reputation at each town.
 - Birds (and bird strikes), weather with rain/snow, seasons, frozen lakes,
   night lights at towns, wrecks you can salvage.
 - Co-op flying (K to hand over controls).

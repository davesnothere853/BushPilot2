# BUSH PILOT 2 — one-page design

**What it is.** A do-anything sandbox set in a made-up mountain valley in the late 1980s. You start broke, with a beater bush plane and an old pickup, and build a living from jobs, missions and your own claims. Everything is a real machine you climb into and work by hand.

**The feel.** Truck-level realism, not plane-level fuss: every machine works the way the real one does and looks like the real reference photos. The look is a crisp, sharp, low-poly world with real wear on everything. You hear real engines, see real smoke on a cold start, and feel the cold morning hard start.

## Core loop
1. **Take work.** Pager jobs: fly freight and people to strips and gravel bars, haul with the trucks, move dirt with the machines, mine your claim.
2. **Earn and spend.** Fuel, parts, repairs, wages; buy better planes, trucks and machines; grow a small shipping/contracting outfit.
3. **Keep it running.** Things wear, break and leak: dirt and wash, cold starts, damaged lines that bleed oil, tyres, filters.
4. **Explore.** The valley: river and sand bars, creeks, rounded forested mountains, the town at the crossroads.

## The world
- **The valley** (hand-made): wide braided river with pale bars, creeks, boulders, a gravel bar where bush planes land, fall colours with leaves that drop and blow.
- **The town**: one main intersection with an 80s gas station, corner store, pharmacy, diner, grocery, police, town hall, post office, fire hall, bank, hardware, barber, tavern, park, burger stand, motel, church, rail crossing, houses; litter and paper that blow in the wind.
- **Ground you can change**: dig, grade, push and pile anywhere; gold in the pay gravels.

## Machines (every one built to the same standard)
Super Cub bush plane · K10 pickup and Blazer · water and fuel trucks · L-95 wheel loader · D-6 dozer with ripper · E-225 excavator · F-50 forklift · tug · log trailer.
- **Real working parts.** Every cylinder is a working hydraulic ram on real clevises and pins. It strokes like a real one and is fed by hard lines from a pump the engine turns: throttle up to lift, it stalls on the relief valve, and a cracked line leaks and the load sinks.
- **Real linkages.** The loader's Z-bar, the excavator's bucket links, the dozer's ripper parallelogram and the forklift's tilting two-stage mast.
- **Real details.** Tracks with sprockets and idlers that turn with the chain, air-suspension seats, lights, rain-cap flaps, cold starts with coughs and a smoke cloud, washable dirt over permanent scratches.

## How we build (Dave's rules)
- **Reference first.** Get real photos, several angles of the exact part, *before* building it.
- **Movement first.** Pivots, mounts and joints first, checked through their whole range. Then shape, then finish.
- **Every part has a job.** No cylinder, line or bracket just for looks. Nothing floats and nothing clips.
- **Built in the lab.** Work live in the Model Lab so it's seen, with a screenshot every push. When Dave circles his mouse around something, that's a problem to fix.
- **Real sounds.** Use real recordings, not generated ones (machines: a real loader cold start, CC0).
- **Ground rules.** No real brand names. Ask before any download. Backups only at milestones. Keep the design notes current.

# Bush Pilot 2 - Player Guide

A 1988 northern bush-flying game: fly freight and passengers between gravel strips, mine a gold claim, log and build, drive the machines, and do it all with friends in co-op. It plays fully on keyboard and mouse or on an Xbox-style controller. Every key can be changed in Menu > Controls and controller.

**To play:** keep the game's .exe, its .pck and the libvoxel .dll together in one folder and run the .exe. Windows may warn about an unknown app: More info > Run anyway.

## 1. Menus and settings

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu (pause) | Esc or F10 | Start |
| Quick menu (switches, lights, map, jobs, controls handoff) | - | LB (in a plane or machine) |
| Help / control reminder | H | D-pad right |
| Full screen | F11 | - |
| Back to the last airstrip | Backspace | Menu > Start over at the last airstrip |
| Console (cheats and tests) | F1 or ` | - |

- **Start** carries on. **Travel** jumps to any airstrip or dock. **Plane load and fuel** sets cargo and fuel.
- **Save slots** (5) save and load games. **New game** wipes everything and starts fresh.
- **Season, weather and realism**: map (Alaska or Outback), season, time of day, wind, weather and passing fronts, sim-lite or realistic flying, controller rumble strength and engine pulses, gore, machine tips, the getting-started list, sandbox (free money) or career.
- **Graphics**: resolution, window mode, quality presets, shadows, render scale.
- **Controls and controller**: rebind any action (two keys and one controller button each).
- The **Getting started** list has an X to close it (click it when the mouse is free, or turn it off in Options).

How to take off: press I for an automatic start (or work the switches by hand), full throttle, keep straight with the rudder, ease the nose up at about 55 knots. Land gently: hard landings, overspeed and crashes damage the plane and cut job pay.

## 4. Tools and weapons

Hold a tool by picking it on the hotbar (1-8 or the wheel; LB / RB on a controller). E or RT uses it.

| Item | Where to get it | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shovel | Tool yard by the hangar | E at the ground or a drift face: digs a shovelful. Swing at another player in co-op. |
| Miner's pick | Tool yard | E at rock or frozen ground: breaks it fast. Heavy hit on players. |
| Gold pan | You start with one | Fill with pay gravel, E at water to pan out the gold. |
| Chainsaw | Always with you | E at a tree fells it (stand clear), E at a fallen trunk bucks it into logs. |
| Pistol | Tool yard (one is left there) | Rides in a holster on your right hip; the first E / RT draws it. Right mouse / LT aims (camera over the shoulder, crosshair); hip shots spray, aimed shots go where the crosshair is. E / RT fires. Leaves bullet holes, breaks glass, damages machines and planes (the part you hit), can take off limbs. |
| Dynamite | Tool yard (a box of 6) | E: against a rock face sets a charge (8 s fuse); looking down sets it at your feet; otherwise throws it (5.5 s). Blasts a crater, throws rock, fells trees. Get clear. |
| Timber sets, rail, ladder, pump, generator, rope lights, gas can | Tool yard | E places or uses them (drift supports, ore-car track, shafts, pumping water out, lighting the drifts, fuelling machines). |

## 5. Trucks and machines

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get in | E at the machine | X |
| Drive / steer | W A S D | RT / LT and left stick |
| Implement up / down (forks, bucket, blade, boom) | Shift / Ctrl | Right stick |
| Engine on / off | I | Quick menu |
| 4WD (2H / 4H / 4L) | G | D-pad up |
| Diff lock / lockers | 9 | Right stick click |
| Control tips on / off | H | D-pad right |
| Get out | O | Hold left stick click |

- **Pickup**: V8, tows trailers, cargo in the bed, hood and tailgate.
- **Water and fuel trucks**: pumps and hoses; wash the plane, fight fires, refuel.
- **Loader**: bucket or forks, front and rear lockers, digs and carries.
- **Dozer**: cuts roads, pads and ramps, pushes trees over.
- **Excavator**: Auto, Boom and Levers modes (Y changes mode), digs into the ground.
- **Drift miner**: tunnels into the claim.
- **Forklift and tug**: pallets, and towing the plane by its tailwheel.
- Watch out: machines run people over and trees fall on them.

## 6. Gold mining

1. Take the shovel and pick from the tool yard (M shows where).
2. Dig pay gravel at the claim (the creek, the knoll, the bog), or blast rock with dynamite.
3. Pan it at water with the gold pan, or run the sluice and the wash plant for volume.
4. Tunnel with timber sets to hold the roof; lay rail for ore cars; pump flooded holes dry; hang rope lights from the generator.
5. Pour gold bars at the workbench in the hangar and sell dust or bars at an assay office (bars pay 8% more). Finds (old coins, bottles, a bison skull, mammoth tusks) sell there too.

## 7. Logging and building

- Fell trees with the chainsaw (E at a tree). One in ten comes back at you: step clear.
- Buck the trunk into logs (E at the fallen tree), carry them or haul them with the pickup and trailer.
- B opens the build menu: lay out an airstrip, put up a log cabin, a hangar or a camp. Logs are the material.

## 8. Jobs, money and planes

- Job board (J, or the menu) at every strip: freight, groceries, mail, fuel drums, medical runs, passengers (walk them to the plane), skydivers, pallets in the DC-3, dynamite for the mines (big pay; a hard landing or a crash sets it off) and core samples (fragile, urgent).
- Rough landings, damage and lateness cut the pay. Repairs, fuel and parts cost money in career mode; sandbox mode makes everything free.
- Planes: the Wolverine bush plane, the Meadowlark biplane, the DC-3. Buy more at Port Ashby and the Proving Ground. Paint, parts and tuning at the workshop.
- Gear for rough fields: tundra tyres, skis and floats.

## 9. The world, damage and gore

- Seasons, time of day, weather fronts that come and go (the radio warns you), wind and turbulence. Two maps: the northern bush and the Outback.
- Crashes bend wings and tear parts off, fuel leaks pool and can catch fire, sparking wires need the master switch off, and fires spread.
- Gore (Options) is on by default: crashes, propellers, machines, falling trees, gunshots, tools and blasts can take arms, legs and heads. Turn it off in Options.
- Controller rumble follows the engine's firing, bumps, crashes and blasts.

## 10. Co-op

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ride along in a friend's plane | E at their door (stopped) | X |
| Get out of their plane | O | Hold left stick click |
| Ask for / hand over the flight controls | K | Quick menu (LB) |
| Fight with a tool | E with a tool in hand, facing them | RT |
| Shoot | Right mouse aim, E fire | LT aim, RT fire |

- **Host**: Menu > Co-op > Host a game. The game tries to open UDP port 24680 on your router and shows the address to give your friend. If they can't connect, forward UDP 24680 to your PC in your router.
- **Join**: Menu > Co-op, type the host's address, Join. You're moved into the host's world (same map, claim and gold); your plane parks beside theirs.
- The host sets the map, season, time and weather. A joining player's menu can't change them or wipe saves.
- Everyone flies their own plane; dynamite lit by one player goes off for everyone.
- Both players need this exact build.
- Not shared yet: trucks, digging, buildings, jobs and money.

## 11. Console (F1)

Type `help` for everything. Useful ones:

- `goto strip / door / creek / pond / knoll / bog / pad / vein / forest` - walk-in teleports
- `tp <strip>`, `strips` - fly to an airstrip
- `give <item> [n]` - e.g. `give pistol`, `give dynamite 6`
- `money <n>`, `sandbox on/off`, `repair`, `fuel <gallons>`
- `weather clear/overcast/rain/fog`, `time <hour>`, `season`, `wind 0-3`
- `buddy` - a stand-in second player for trying co-op alone (`buddy fly / land / ask / come / stay / fight / peace / off`)
- `handcheck <item>` - a close look at what's in your hands (`aim`, `side`, `off`)
- `perf` / `perf off` - frame-time readout; `rumble` - tests the controller

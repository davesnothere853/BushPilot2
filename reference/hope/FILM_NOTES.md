# Hope, 1982 - notes from a "then & now" filming-locations video (watched 13 Oct)
Reference only, for building the 1982 town. In the game: no film names, characters,
logos or real brand names - every sign generic.

## Look of the town in 1982
- Overcast, wet, misty spring: low cloud on the mountains, wet asphalt, grey light.
- Mountains loom straight behind the main street - steep, forested, snow on the tops
  (the peak south of town fills the end of Wallace St).
- Wide main streets, faded white/yellow lines, angle/curb parking, low 1-2 storey
  commercial blocks, flat roofs, big tall pole signs above the roofline.
- Lots of overhead wires on wooden poles; tall curved-arm street lights; traffic lights
  hung over the intersection (red/amber/green on a span).

## Spots seen (1982 / now)
1. Highway into town, rock cut on one side, guard rail, two-lane - a 1970s orange/yellow
   pickup (cab-over boxy truck) passing.
2. Welcome arch over the road: big log uprights with year numbers carved down them,
   a log crossbeam, a red "WELCOME TO..." board under it (make it generic: "WELCOME").
3. Parking lot by the station: rows of late-60s/70s sedans, a boxy 4x4 wagon, pickups.
4. Wallace St at the main corner: a two-storey cream/pale-yellow building with flat roof,
   a shopping plaza with a tall pole sign ("SHOPPING PLAZA" style), a savings/bank
   storefront with a red sign band, a drug store with big painted holiday window
   displays (snowmen, trees - painted on the glass), deep window ledges.
5. Police car of the era: big 2-door sedan, white body with dark blue doors/hood
   (two-tone), single red rotating beacon on the roof plus a bar, push bar.
6. 3rd Ave looking down the street: a two-storey gable-front shop (beige stucco,
   small upper windows, a little shed-roof porch over the door), a long low strip
   with a pole sign, a motel sign and a "BURGERS" sign, a hardware store front.
7. The motel: two-storey, long, white with orange/brown vertical panels, open
   balcony walkway along the upper floor, exterior stairs, lawn and low hedge in front.
8. Gas station: island with two old pumps (white boxes, round-shouldered), canopy
   posts, a two-storey yellow/cream block behind with a fire escape.
9. Burger/ice-cream stand -> now a pizza place: low building, red standing-seam roof,
   white walls, small windows; a "MOVING VAN" rental sign out front.
10. Rail crossing on the edge of town: ballast and ties, crossing signal box,
    small houses and firs behind, fog on the hills.
11. Residential corner: white wood-frame gabled house with trim, picket fence,
    a hydrant at the curb, big old maples, low cloud.
12. Old truss bridge over the river (steel through-truss, grey, wooden deck,
    concrete piers) - replaced in 2011 with a plain modern one; rocky braided river.
13. Back roads: two-lane, yellow centre line, gravel shoulders, fence posts, fields.
14. Bush: mossy rock slabs, cedar/fir forest, ferns, old mine / logging junk -
    a rusted boxy truck body with big fenders, rusty machinery, old drums.
15. Lake cabins: weathered wood cabins on a lakeshore, grassy slope, dock.
